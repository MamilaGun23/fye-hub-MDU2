YOUR FYE SUPPORT GUIDE

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers and practical examples for students, mentors, and supervisors.

Use Sign in with your email and password. FYE Hub opens the dashboard for your saved account role and active mentor appointment automatically. Existing sessions resume when you return. If you followed an event attendance link, sign in to continue to that event.

Students

How do I find my mentor?

Open My mentor in your dashboard after a supervisor has assigned one. If no mentor is shown yet, check again later or contact your FYE coordinator.

How can I contact my mentor?

Use Messages for a private FYE conversation, or choose the email or WhatsApp option on the mentor profile when it is available.

Who can apply to become a mentor?

Mentor applications open October 1 through January 31 for all students. First-year applicants can submit before final results, then complete their evidence later. Approval waits until the following year, verified progression to Year 2 and new-year registration. New January entrants wait until October. Returning applicants provide latest academic results and current registration proof.

Example message: “Hi, I’m finding it difficult to locate the library. Could you tell me which building it is near?”

Mentors

Can I change a student’s profile or assignment?

No. Mentor dashboards are read-only for student records. Contact your supervisor if an assignment or student detail needs changing.

How do I request an event?

Open Calendar, select a date, then request approval. Include a clear title, purpose, start time, and venue. The event is shared after a supervisor approves it.

How do I log first contact with a student?

Choose Log first contact in My students. Messages opens with that student selected and an editable introduction. Review it and choose Send message to send the introduction and record contact. Your unsent drafts stay with their selected student.

Where are my older event requests?

Current requests appear with the nearest event first. Open Past and closed requests to see older, declined or cancelled requests and any outcome forms.

Event example: “Study skills check-in” · Purpose: “Share note-taking and exam-planning tips” · 14:00 · Student Centre, Room 2.

Profile bio example: “I’m a third-year student who enjoys helping new students find their feet. Ask me about campus services and study routines.”

Supervisors

How do I find a student in the directory?

Search by name, email or student number. You can enter name parts in any order. Select a faculty to narrow the programme choices; dropdown changes apply immediately. Open More filters for a mentor, assigned or unassigned status, entry status and study year. Use Clear filters to return to the full directory.

How do I display mentor applicants?

Open Mentor applications and use the search and dropdown filters to narrow both lists by applicant, status, school, programme, study year or target academic year. Choose Show mentor applications or Show shortlisted candidates to open each list. Use Clear filters to show all applications again. Wide tables scroll sideways to reveal all details.

How are students assigned fairly?

Use Balance all unassigned students to distribute eligible students across approved mentors in the selected faculty and programme, while respecting each mentor’s capacity.

When do overdue contact alerts appear?

The supervisor inbox flags active assignments with no mentor contact logged within seven days.

Assignment example: Filter students by programme, then use the balanced assignment action or select a mentor and choose students within the remaining capacity.

Admin account and programme review

Why can the same name appear under different programmes?

Different people can share a name. Use the student number to identify the account. User accounts, account details and programme account lists show that identifier. Search the number to find the specific account.

How do I browse existing programmes?

In Faculties & programmes, open Browse existing programmes, then open a faculty or search by programme or faculty name. Rename, delete and qualification controls remain under each programme’s three-dot menu.

Events and updates

Where can I find past and upcoming events?

Open Calendar and select a date. Event cards show whether an event is upcoming or past, along with its time and venue. Supervisors can also filter by event status.

Can I change an event notification back to unread?

Yes. Open Notifications and use Mark unread on any update or event notification.

Good update example: “Orientation venue change” · “The Friday welcome session has moved from the Main Hall to the Student Centre. The start time is unchanged.”
